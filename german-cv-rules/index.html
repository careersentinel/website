CV · Lebenslauf

German CV rules, from the official guides

A German CV (Lebenslauf) has rules nobody spells out. These come from two public guides by the Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Germany's federal employment agency: one in German, one in English for people applying from abroad. I read both on 23 November 2026. Where they differ, I say so.

The structure

  • Newest firstStart with your current or latest job and work backwards.Both guides
  • Month and year for every entryA start and an end, like 03/2021 to 05/2023, for every job and for your education.Both guides
  • Two pages at mostA CV should not be longer than two pages.English guide
  • About three to five bullet points per jobName the employer and your position, then say briefly what you did.English guide
  • Date and signature at the endThe German guide says: remember place, date and a handwritten signature. The English guide says to end with the date and your signature.Both guides

Contact and personal details

  • Contact details firstBoth guides put them at the beginning. The German guide lists phone and email. Applying from abroad? The English guide says to use the correct country code.Both guides
  • A photo is not mandatoryEmployers may no longer demand one, says the German guide, but it is still entirely usual. The English guide says most employers still expect one.Both guides
  • Marital status and religion are not requiredThat is what the English guide says. It still includes your date and place of birth. The German guide also lists marital status and nationality under personal details.The guides differ

Gaps, languages, soft skills

  • Short gaps need no explanationGaps between two jobs shorter than three months need no explanation, says the German guide. The English guide says to explain gaps yourself, in the cover letter or the interview.The guides differ
  • Languages: your highest certificate is enoughList your language certificates. One per language, the highest, is enough.English guide
  • Soft skills are better in the cover letterTeamwork, resilience and the like do not need a list on the CV.English guide

The file

  • Same font throughout, nothing playfulThe German guide says the whole application in one font. The English guide says avoid playful fonts and keep the formatting consistent.Both guides
  • By email: one PDF, 3 MB at mostCover letter, CV and certificates go into a single PDF file.German guide
