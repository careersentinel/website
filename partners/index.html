What each person gets

People sign up on the website, upload their CV once and set their German level. Tap a card for the details.

A short list every morning

By email or Telegram. Every job has a match score and the reason behind it.

How it works

Only the few worth applying to

Each morning it checks the new ads against their CV and sends the few that fit. Every job gets a score from 0 to 100 and a short reason. On a quiet day it says so, instead of filling the list.

The German the job really needs

On the A1 to C2 scale, next to their own level.

How it works

It reads between the lines

Phrases like "sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse" get placed on the A1 to C2 scale and shown next to the person's own level. When the ad doesn't say, it tells them it can't tell, instead of guessing.

Relocation traps flagged

"German market" jobs that are really based in another country get a warning.

How it works

Listed in Germany, based elsewhere

Some ads show up in German job searches but the team sits in another country. When the ad gives that away, the job gets a clear warning, so nobody plans around the wrong job.

A CV and letter per job

Written only from facts in their own CV. They always press send.

How it works

Tailored, never invented

For a job on their list they can get a CV and cover letter written for that role. It only uses what's in their own CV and never sends anything. They read it, change it and decide.

A Werkstudent track

Students can switch the search to student jobs.

How it works

Built for studying and working

Students can switch to the Werkstudent track. Then the search looks for Werkstudent jobs instead of full-time roles, and internships that require enrolment stay away from people who aren't students.

A free German CV check

Their CV checked against what German employers expect.

How it works

Concrete tips, not a grade

A CV that works abroad often misses what German employers look for. The check says what to change, in plain words, so the next application starts stronger.

FAQ

Questions partners ask

What does a pilot cost?

A fixed price, agreed with you before it starts. It depends on the size of the group and how long it runs. The people you support don't pay anything.

Will it replace our counsellors?

No. It does the searching and the first filtering, so your counsellors have more time to talk with people.

Does it give visa advice?

No. It flags facts in the ad, like a job that is really based abroad. Visa and residence questions belong with the Ausländerbehörde or a migration counselling service.

Is it only for internationals?

No, it's for everyone job hunting in Germany. The German check matters most for people whose first language isn't German.

Where do the jobs come from?

From public job databases and over 3,000 company job boards across Germany, searched again every morning. Only sources that allow it.
